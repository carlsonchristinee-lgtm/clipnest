# ClipNest
### Where Everything Has Its Place

ClipNest is your personal clipboard library. Keep your copied text, links, images, and important information organized in one convenient place.

## Features
- Save and organize clips.
- Create custom folders.
- Search your saved content.
- Manage and delete clips.
- Backup and restore your collection.

## Privacy and Storage
Your clips are stored locally in your browser on your device. They are not automatically synced between devices.

## Getting Started
ClipNest should be hosted using HTTPS or a local web server for clipboard features to work correctly.

## Project
ClipNest is designed to make saving, finding, and organizing copied information easier.
